Decaffeinate GitHub ↗

Close the lid.
Keep working.

A tiny menu bar app that keeps your MacBook awake with the lid closed, in Low Power Mode with the display off, so AI agents and long builds keep running. Open the lid and everything goes back.

Download for Mac View source

Free · MIT · macOS 13+ · Apple silicon & Intel

1

Approve once

On first launch, one admin prompt adds a sudoers rule that allows only pmset sleep and Low Power changes.

2

Close the lid

The Mac stays awake, switches to Low Power Mode and turns the display off. Your agents keep going.

3

Open it

Your exact battery and charger settings come back. Nothing to undo.

It never stays awake unattended. Heat, a low battery, quit, logout or a crash all let the Mac sleep again.

Decaffeinate is a fork of Pilot Light by sudolabs (MIT). The idea and the original design are theirs; this fork restructured the code, fixed bugs and simplified setup. If you find it useful, support this fork on Ko-fi or sponsor the original author.
